# Phase 1 — Triage

Decide whether a specific posting is worth an application. Read
`assets/positioning.md` first if the asset bank and the weaknesses are not
already in context. Do not restate facts from there — cite them.

## Output format

Verdict first. Whole thing under 200 words unless he asks for depth.

```
VERDICT: apply / apply-with-outreach-first / monitor / skip
P(interview): <number>% — <the one thing that drives it>
Expected band: $<low>–<high> (source: <where the number came from>)
Credential gap: <does this posting hard-require what he lacks?>
Lead artifact: <A1 / A2 / A3 / A4 — which one this JD is actually about>
Blocker to clear first: <usually "publish the repo", sometimes "find the PI">
```

## Kill criteria, in order

### K1. Hard credential floor
Does the posting state a master's/PhD as **required** (not "preferred")?
- Required, no named human inside → **skip**, unless it is Tier 1 in
  `positioning.md`, in which case → **apply-with-outreach-first**.
- Preferred → not a kill. This is the tier where the artifacts compete.
- Not mentioned → strongest tier; prioritize.

### K2. Domain distance
How far is this from children's residential care, child welfare, trafficking
measurement, or administrative-records analysis?
- Direct → apply.
- Adjacent public-interest data (justice, health services, housing) → apply,
  lead with method rather than domain.
- Generic corporate analytics → **skip**. He is not competing there and the
  artifacts do not translate.

### K3. Data access
Does the role hand him access to data he cannot get alone (NCANDS, AFCARS
restricted, state administrative files, a hotline dataset)?
- Yes → upgrade the verdict one level. This is the single biggest thing an
  employer can offer him and it should be named in the cover letter.

### K4. Excluded categories
For-profit consumer-platform trust and safety; anything in Tamaulipas
attaching his name to organized-crime or trafficking analytics; sales
engineering in any form. → **skip**, no discussion.

### K5. Comp
**Never a kill criterion.** He has said he will take what these roles pay.
Report the band factually so he is not surprised, and move on. Do not import
the retired SE floors.

### K6. Location
**Never a kill criterion.** US on-site, hybrid and relocation are all fine.

## Estimating P(interview) honestly

Start from a realistic base and adjust. Do not inflate to be encouraging — he
has explicitly said inflated estimates destroy the value of the advice.

| Situation | Rough base |
|---|---|
| Cold application, MA required, no contact | 2–5% |
| Cold application, MA preferred, artifacts public | 10–15% |
| Cold application, MA preferred, artifacts **not** public | 5–8% |
| Warm — a named researcher has replied to him | 30–50% |
| Grant-funded post where the PI wants him specifically | 50%+ |

The gap between rows 2 and 3 is the entire argument for publishing. Say so.

## When he pastes a JD or URL

1. Extract: required vs preferred credentials, the actual data the role
   touches, whether a writing sample is requested, whether the team has
   published anything he has read.
2. Map to one lead artifact. If none of the four fit, that is itself a signal
   the posting is off-target.
3. If the team has published in this space, **search for their prior work
   before writing the verdict** — knowing their papers changes both the
   verdict and the outreach, and A2 is specifically a conversation about five
   named published studies.
4. Give the verdict block. Offer the CV pass; do not run it unasked.

## Do not

- Do not recommend applying to more than a handful of things at once. His
  advantage is depth of engagement with a specific team's work, and that does
  not scale to volume applications.
- Do not describe the artifacts as published while they are local.
- Do not soften the credential gap. A hiring manager will not.
